# Leadership photos

Drop square photos here (JPG or PNG, roughly 400×400, under ~200 KB),
then point at them from `src/lib/leadership.ts`:

    photo: '/leadership/manojkumar-deshpande.jpg',

Until a photo is added the site shows a styled initials badge, so the
section looks finished either way.

Use photos the institute owns or has permission to publish — a headshot
from the PIEMR site is normally fine; a photo found elsewhere on the web
is normally not.
